The goal of this project was to identify where the company could focus its growth efforts. Overall, the dataset provided a variety of variables involving financial performance, staffing, and clinical information, including specialties and procedural information for each specific clinic. The data was initially cleaned in Python, where the analysis was also originally planned to be conducted. However, given the limited number of quantitative variables, Tableau was used for the analysis instead. The report was divided into three areas: financial performance, clinical observations, and operational bandwidth, to identify multiple potential areas for growth and give stakeholders flexibility in evaluating opportunities.
